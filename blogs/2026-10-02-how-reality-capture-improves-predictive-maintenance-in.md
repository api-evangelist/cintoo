Unplanned downtime is still the most expensive problem on many industrial sites. A single unexpected failure on a critical asset can cost more in a few hours than an entire year of planned maintenance. That's why reliability teams have spent the last decade moving from reactive and calendar-based maintenance toward predictive strategies built on real condition data.
