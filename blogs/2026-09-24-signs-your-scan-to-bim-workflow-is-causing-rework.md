Rework on industrial projects often traces back to one place - the way your team handles scan data between capture and BIM modeling. When reality capture data sits unused or gets manually translated into design models, errors compound. Misaligned pipe runs, missed structural elements, and coordination conflicts show up during fabrication or installation, rather than during planning.
