Industrial sites generate more data than ever, yet most teams still struggle to turn that data into decisions. Laser scans, BIM models, sensor feeds, maintenance records and engineering documents pile up in separate systems and the people who need them - operators, engineers, maintenance crews, safety leads and executives - rarely see the same picture at the same time.
